## Q: Where does the data come from?
A: Multiple sources: official site info, user feedback, automated detection scripts. All data is public and transparent.

## Q: Are invite links safe?
A: Yes, all invite links point to official registration pages. Registration via invite links may offer additional bonuses.

## Q: What happens when a site goes down?
A: Down sites are automatically marked as "dead" and removed from the ranking. If they recover, they are automatically re-listed.

## Q: How do I submit a new site?
A: Two options:
1. Submit via the admin panel (admin.html)
2. Submit a PR on GitHub, editing data/stations_info.json

## Q: How is the score calculated?
A: Based on 5 dimensions: Stability(30%), Speed(25%), Price(20%), Support(15%), Features(10%). See [[璇勫垎鏍囧噯]].
